#
# ── What this machine can and cannot run, measured ────────────────────────
# RTX 3050 Laptop, 6144 MiB VRAM. After the display's ~200 MiB and the planner's
# 1024 MiB headroom, `resources.probe()` reports **4856 MiB usable**. So:
#
#   describe / narrate / style-read   6200 MB (qwen3-vl-8b-awq)   DO NOT FIT
#   narrate-deep                     21500 MB (InternVL3-38B)     DO NOT FIT
#
# `sizing.registry.unrunnable()` says so by name with the reason, before any
# download. That is not a defect — it is why the Engine tab is built around models
# *you* choose and size, not around the Kaggle catalogue. Everything at or under
# ~4.5 GB in AWQ/GGUF-adjacent quantisation is fair game, and sm_86 gives this card
# bf16 and FlashAttention-2 that Kaggle's T4 never had.
#
# RAM is the tighter constraint and it surprises people: 1.6 GB available of 12 GB
# installed while the app is running. `device_map` + `low_cpu_mem_usage` matter more
# here than on any Kaggle kernel.
